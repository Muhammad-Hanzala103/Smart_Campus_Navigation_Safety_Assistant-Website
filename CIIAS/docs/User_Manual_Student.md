# CIIAS - Student Guide

## 1. Getting Started
- **Download App:** Get the Android App from the Google Play Store (Mock).
- **Login:** Use your University Email.
- **Biometrics:** Enable Fingerprint login in "Settings" for faster access.

## 2. Key Features

### 📍 AR Navigation
- Open the "Navigation" tab.
- Point camera at campus buildings.
- Arrows will guide you to your class.

### 🚌 Transport
- Check "Shuttle Tracker" to see where the bus is.
- **ETAs** are updated every 30 seconds.

### 🆘 Emergency (SOS)
- **Shake to Alert:** Strongly shake your phone 3 times to send an SOS.
- **Silent Alert:** Press the "Panic" button on the home screen.
- This shares your **Live Location** with Security.

### 🍔 Smart Cafeteria
1.  Go to "Cafeteria".
2.  Add items (Burgers, Drinks) to cart.
3.  Pay via "Campus Wallet".
4.  Show the **Order QR Code** at the counter.

## 3. Academic Portal
- View your **GPA** and **Attendance** in real-time.
- If attendance is < 75%, you will get a warning notification.

---
**Privacy:** Your location is *only* shared when you enable "Companion Walk" or trigger an SOS.
